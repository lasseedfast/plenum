Semantic discovery over whole debates — entire sessions, not individual speeches — matched
on their generated summaries.

This is a NAVIGATION tool. Use it to locate interesting debates, then call
`fetch_debate(debate_id, query=<the same query>)` to see the speeches inside.

**Do not cite a debate.** Debates are a way in; the citable sources are the individual
speeches you find through them.

For broad thematic questions this is often cheaper than searching speeches directly: find
the two or three debates where the topic was actually argued, then drill in.

Skip this path when the question is about specific individuals, keywords or statistics — use
`search_speeches` or `database_query` instead.
